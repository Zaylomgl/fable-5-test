FAQ

Isn't this what the free CISA emails do?

CISA's alerts are great — and unfiltered. You still have to read everything to find what concerns you. We only send what matches the stack you declared.

How is it prioritized?

CISA KEV (known exploited in the wild) first, then EPSS (probability of exploitation), then CVSS. Severity scores alone are a poor triage signal.

What will it cost?

A free tier (one product, weekly digest), and paid plans around the price of one coffee per week for unlimited stacks and daily alerts. Waitlist members get early-bird pricing.
